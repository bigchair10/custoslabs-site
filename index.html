The problem

Advisors want AI. Compliance says no.

The major providers' terms reserve rights to retain or train on submitted data. For a firm bound by SEC Regulation S-P, FINRA recordkeeping and fiduciary duty of confidentiality, that makes the entire category unusable — not for lack of a product, but because no provider can offer frontier quality and a guarantee that client data never leaves the firm's control.

So the work doesn't get done.

Document review, client correspondence, research synthesis — the work most improved by language models is precisely the work that touches the most confidential material. Firms are left doing it by hand while less-regulated competitors automate.

How it works

  • 01Ingest. Prospectuses, Form ADVs, 10-Ks, advisory agreements and trust instruments are parsed with page-aware chunking, on your hardware.
  • 02Retrieve. Local embedding and a cross-encoder reranker surface the decisive clause — often a single sentence inside a two-hundred-page filing.
  • 03Answer with citations. An open-weight model generates with inline source references tied to the exact page.
  • 04Verify. Every cited span is checked programmatically against the source. Anything that cannot be grounded is flagged rather than asserted.
  • 05Audit. Each query and its sources are recorded in a tamper-evident log, for SEC or FINRA examination.
